*Built for the Feed: How Digital-First Creators Are Building the Next Generation of Entertainment Brands* is a narrative business and pop-culture nonfiction project I developed during Entertainment Media Publishing and Distribution at Full Sail University.

## The situation

Digital creators can now build an audience before receiving access to a publisher, studio, network, or major sponsor. The harder problem is turning that attention into a business that can survive beyond one platform or viral moment. I wanted to examine that problem through gaming, esports, and creator-led entertainment.

## The task

I needed to build a professional book proposal that proved a business case for the project. The proposal had to identify a clear audience, explain why the book belongs in the market, show how I could support promotion, and provide enough sample material to demonstrate the writing direction.

## What I created

The completed proposal includes:

- A project overview and market position
- Primary and secondary audience profiles
- An author platform and promotional plan
- Competitive analysis of four related books
- A twelve-chapter table of contents
- Three sample chapters

The book uses Tempest Esports, a concept-stage organization developed through my graduate coursework, as a transparent working case. I do not present Tempest as a finished company or claim results it has not produced. Instead, each chapter separates completed planning from the evidence that would still be needed before launch.

## Publishing and distribution research

I also compared Kindle Direct Publishing, IngramSpark, and Lulu as possible print-on-demand services. IngramSpark offered the broadest access to bookstore and library ordering systems, while Lulu provided flexible book formats and global distribution options. I selected KDP as the best starting point for this project because simplicity was my main priority. Its workflow keeps digital publishing, print-on-demand production, Amazon sales, and optional expanded distribution in one system.

## Result

The result is a complete book proposal with a defined audience, market position, promotional direction, chapter structure, and sample content. The proposal is finished, but the full manuscript is still in development. No publication, sales, or audience-growth results are claimed.

## Reflection

This project changed how I think about publishing. A strong idea is only one part of the decision. The author also needs a defined reader, a realistic path to distribution, a promotional plan, and a clear reason the project belongs beside existing books. The proposal forced me to connect creative direction with the business decisions required to release and support the work.

## Next step

The next step is to complete the remaining manuscript chapters, test the book's positioning with its intended audience, and decide whether traditional representation or self-publishing provides the better release path.
